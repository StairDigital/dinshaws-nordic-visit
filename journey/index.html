    Top 10 Insights

    The visit confirmed that Dinshaw's has a clear entry point in drinkable and high-protein yoghurt, and that the requirements list for the line is largely ready.

    1. Drinkable is the white spaceLassi is the mass-market route in; drinkable yoghurt is the premium layer once consumers are ready.
    2. Protein is the global premium driverValio, Lindahls and Danone lead with 25 to 26 g per pack, and India's vegetarian protein gap makes the case stronger.
    3. India's yoghurt journey runs in reverseGlobally plain moves to drinkable then spoonable; India starts with set dahi and needs its own playbook.
    4. Naming drives adoptionProbiotic curd, high-protein skyr and lassi are clearer labels than "yoghurt".
    5. Tetra Top fits the shelf-life ambition45 to 60 days below 8°C without an aseptic line; the real test in India is the cold chain.
    6. Design for 60 days, declare less45 days at 4°C, and about 15 days at 10 to 12°C once product leaves Dinshaw's control.
    7. Inclusions carry a large premiumChilled yoghurt with pieces sells about 60% above plain in China, and few Tetra Top products carry pieces yet.
    8. Size the plant for 120%Protein concentration rarely delivers paper yields, so capacity is set after the process is frozen.
    9. Capex closer to Rs 30 to 34 CrOnce building, Bactofuge and filling are included; to be validated with supplier quotes.
    10. Cheese and ice cream formats are structural growthCheddar is expected to double by 2030; extrusion shapes are hard to copy, inclusions are not.

    Dinshaw's Process Flows, Written in Sweden

    In the night session Mr Zervin Rana walked through the draft product slate. All 4 flows share 1 front end; STAIR later turned them into factory flow charts.

    Dahi

    Fat 3.5% · Protein 4.5% · RO
    1. Bactofuge, homogenise, pasteurise
    2. Chill to 45°C
    3. Add culture
    4. Fill in cups
    5. Incubate in the cup
    6. Blast chill
    7. Store at 4°C

    Greek Yoghurt

    Protein 7–8% · Fat under 2%
    1. Pre-concentrate by RO or UF
    2. Ferment
    3. Break the curd
    4. Strain whey
    5. Add sugar and fruit paste
    6. Thermise
    7. Fill with fruit dosing

    Skyr

    Protein 10–12% · Fat under 1%
    1. Skimmed milk, Bactofuge, pasteurise
    2. Add culture and rennet
    3. Ferment
    4. Strain whey
    5. Whisk smooth
    6. Thermise
    7. Fill cups, fruit dice added

    Lassi

    Recipe-based standardisation
    1. Bactofuge, homogenise, pasteurise
    2. Chill to 45°C
    3. Ferment
    4. Break curd, add sugar
    5. Thermise
    6. Add flavour
    7. Fill
